# Renatura Normandie — 5-minute pitch

## 0:00–0:45 — The question

Where should Normandy restore ecological connections first? A high-scoring
place is not necessarily the best collection of places when resources are limited.
Decision-makers need both transparent priorities and a way to compare sets of sites.

## 0:45–1:45 — Demonstration

Open the real Pays d’Auge pilot. Inspect a cell, show its observed 2010 and 2016
hedgerow lengths, and distinguish the two available indicators from missing
pressure and feasibility. Toggle the historical layer, then inspect the modelled
connections. Explicitly explain that these are planning cells, not ready-to-buy parcels.

## 1:45–3:00 — The decision

Choose the five-site scenario. Compare its combined objective with the naive
same-budget score ranking. Change the envelope and run the browser search.
Explain the difference between a proven model optimum and a feasible solution.
The cost proxy is hectares, not euros; modelled gain is not measured biodiversity.

## 3:00–4:00 — Trust and reproducibility

Show source attribution and the Monte Carlo stability panel. Every deployed
measurement is derived from documented real data. The full source and snapshots
can be rebuilt with one command. Heavy analysis happens offline; the app is static
and can operate on an iPad without a compute backend or tracking.

## 4:00–5:00 — Impact and next steps

The pilot makes the reasoning inspectable and supports more informed discussion.
Next: validate pressure and opportunity layers, calibrate connectivity with an
ecologist, compare three known situations and measure tablet performance.
Extend to Normandy only after coverage checks. Ask for expertise, verified open
layers and user testing rather than claiming validated ecological impacts.

## Likely questions

- Are these real costs? No, hectare proxies.
- Is the solution ecologically optimal? Only optimal for the stated model.
- Why missing criteria? They remain visibly null until a suitable source is validated.
- Why not Spark? The pilot fits local vector processing; a distributed cluster adds overhead.
- Can the iPad update all raw data? No; the offline pipeline runs on a computer.
